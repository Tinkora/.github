# Maintainers

## Current responsibility

As of 2026-08-09, the `tinkora` GitHub organization has one owner and one
member. The administrative maintainer is `@tinkeragora`. Closed
`maintainers` and `security` Teams exist, but both contain only this same
operator. No independent reviewer, release Team, or effective remote
CODEOWNERS file exists.

The single-owner state is a known continuity risk. It does not authorize a
second account controlled by the same person as a substitute for an
independent, trusted owner.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Keep project scope, lifecycle state, and public claims accurate.
- Triage contributions and resolve review conversations.
- Run and record project checks before merge or release.
- Apply least privilege and keep sensitive material out of public repositories.
- Coordinate deprecation, incidents, and translations according to the
  policies in [`docs/`](docs/ACCESS_MODEL.md).

## Appointment and removal

Project maintainer access requires sustained, trustworthy contributions, a
clear ongoing responsibility, and explicit organization-owner approval.
Organization owner access additionally requires independent account recovery,
secure 2FA, and willingness to perform continuity duties.

Access is reviewed when responsibilities change, a maintainer becomes inactive,
or a security concern arises. Removal should preserve contributor credit and
record the public role transition without publishing private reasons.

The current Team evidence and future CODEOWNERS model are documented in
[docs/ACCESS_MODEL.md](docs/ACCESS_MODEL.md). Team creation does not satisfy
the independent-owner or non-author-review gates.
